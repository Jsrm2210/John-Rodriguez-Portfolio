Case study · NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy · Fall 2025

Nova Aeros: a rover for Martian lava-tube caves

Our 19-person team designed a Discovery-class mission to send a solar-powered rover into collapsed lava tubes in the Tharsis region. The rover would map cave interiors and measure temperature, radiation, gas gradients and mineral content, both to find environments that could preserve signs of life and to judge whether caves could shelter future crews.

As Deputy Project Manager of Resources I worked on the programmatics: schedule and budget bases of estimate, risk tracking and weekly status to the Project Manager, keeping the design inside the client's limits.
